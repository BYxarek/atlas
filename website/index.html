01 / STRATEGY

Every conquest starts with a decision.

01

Build your advantage

Upgrade businesses to fund your next move. Recruit infantry, aircraft and ships, then research technologies that make each unit stronger.

02

Know before you go

Send a spy for an army snapshot. Weigh the cost of an overseas operation. Attack warnings can be rumors — intelligence matters.

03

Make your move

Watch operations cross the map and battles resolve. Ten first-time conquests earn a nuclear charge. Hold territory and build your empire.

02 / NATIVE

One campaign. Room to move.

Touch, drag and pinch on Android. A wide map on desktop. Portrait and landscape layouts, English and Russian, local saves and sound effects.

The main menu puts actions on the left and the map, installed version and GitHub project link on the right. Settings has Interface, Display and Audio pages. When a newer stable release is available, the game shows its notes and adds a shortcut to the latest release.

03 / MULTIPLAYER

Your friends. Your server.

Up to 176 humans and bots share a room. The server owns the simulation and saves; Windows and Android players use the same protocols.

LAN

Host from a client on your local network. Discover nearby rooms or connect by address.
